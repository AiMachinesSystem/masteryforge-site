Privacy Policy

This is a static website with two measurement tools on it: Google Analytics and the Meta pixel. They count visits and help our advertising. Nothing else on these pages collects anything. Here is the whole story, and it is short.

  • Effective · 2026-07-26
  • This site · analytics + ad pixel only
  • Checkout · handled by Shopify

01The short version

This site is a folder of static files — HTML, CSS and a few kilobytes of JavaScript, served as-is. There is no account system and no database of visitors on our side. Two third-party measurement scripts run on these pages (listed below, with opt-outs); everything else you can read without giving us anything at all.

02What this site collects

Two measurement tools, and only those:

  • Google Analytics (GA4). Counts visits and pages viewed so we know which plans people care about. It sets its own cookies. Same measurement property as our Shopify store, so a visit here and a purchase there read as one journey. Opt out any time with Google's browser add-on: tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout.
  • Meta (Facebook) pixel. Tells our advertising which visits came from an ad, so we stop paying for ads that don't work. It sets its own cookies. You can control how Meta uses this in your Meta ad preferences, or block it with any standard content blocker: the site works fine with it blocked.
  • No forms. There is nothing to fill in on this site, so nothing you type ever reaches us from here.
  • No session recording. No heatmaps, no keystroke capture, no fingerprinting beyond what the two tools above do by their own policies. Our own JavaScript fades sections in as you scroll and shows a buy bar on small screens. It talks to nobody.

03Where your purchase data goes

When you click a buy button you leave this site and land on the checkout of our Shopify store. Everything you enter there — name, address, payment details — is collected and processed by Shopify under its own privacy policy, not this page. We never see your card number.

Your checkout and account data is governed by the store's privacy policy: shop.masteryforgecrafts.com/policies/privacy-policy. This marketing site collects no personal data at all — the two policies stay consistent because there is nothing on this side to govern.

04If you email us

If you write to us — for a refund, a question, anything — we of course receive your email address and what you wrote. We use it only to answer you. There is no newsletter you didn't ask for and no list that gets sold or shared. The fastest route is replying to your order confirmation email; it reaches a human.

05Server logs

Like almost every website, the host that serves these files may keep standard technical logs — IP address, browser type, page requested, time of day. Those logs keep the server running and secure. They are not tied to any profile, and we do not use them to track, follow or re-identify anyone.

06Changes to this page

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes. The principle will not: we measure visits and advertising, and we collect nothing else here. If we ever add a tool beyond the two listed above, it gets named on this page first.

07Questions

Reply to your order confirmation email, or write to the store through its contact channels. A human reads it — usually the same one who dimensioned the plans.
