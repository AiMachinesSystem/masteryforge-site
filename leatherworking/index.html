Choose a project that matches your tools and skills. Leatherworking can involve pattern layout, cutting, making stitch holes, hand stitching and edge finishing. Review the selected project and start with a level of detail that feels manageable.

The collection is planned around wallets, cardholders, bags, pouches, belts and everyday accessories. Project complexity will vary, so choose a pattern around the tools, leather and time you have.

New additions will be listed with the collection when they are available. Update details and access instructions will be confirmed when the product opens for purchase.

Tools depend on your selected project. Hand-cut and hand-stitched leatherwork commonly uses a cutting tool and mat, a ruler, stitch-marking or hole-making tools, needles and thread. Check the chosen pattern before gathering your supplies.

Consider the purpose of the finished item, the pattern and the construction method. Check leather type and thickness, then plan thread, closures, straps or other hardware before buying.
